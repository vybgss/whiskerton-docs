01Who we are

Whiskerton: Idle Cat Cafe (the “App”) is an iOS and iPadOS game developed and published by Volodymyr Sichka, an independent developer (“we”, “us” or “our”). This Privacy Policy applies to the App and to this website. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

02Information we collect

We don't collect any personal information. The App doesn't ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone or any other personal data. It has no user accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't send your data to us or to anyone else. Apart from Apple's StoreKit and the copy of your save in your own iCloud (see section 04), the App makes no network requests, and we run no servers for it, so nothing about you is stored on our side.

03Data stored on your device

So that you can keep playing where you left off, the App saves your game in its own local storage on your device. This data is about the game, not about you, and none of it identifies you. It never reaches us; the only copy outside your device is the one in your own iCloud (see section 04).

Whether you own the Golden Bowl or a legendary cat, or have an active Cat Club subscription, isn't decided by the save: the App reads it from your App Store purchases on the device through Apple's StoreKit.

04iCloud sync

So that your café follows you to your other iPhone and iPad and comes back after you reinstall the App, the App keeps a copy of the save file described above in Apple's iCloud key-value storage, under the Apple Account you're signed in to on the device. The copy holds the same game data and nothing else. It's stored and transferred by Apple as part of iCloud and governed by the Apple Privacy Policy. We can't see or access it, and it isn't used for anything but your game.

Syncing is on by default and needs you to be signed in to iCloud. You can turn it off on any device with iCloud sync in the game's Settings; the App then neither reads nor writes the copy on that device.

05In-app purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases: gem packs, the Golden Bowl, the Starter Pack, five legendary cats and the Cat Club subscription. All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, Apple Account, card or other payment details. The App only receives a confirmation from Apple that a purchase went through, so it can give you what you bought. To show prices and handle purchases, Apple's StoreKit connects to the App Store. Purchases and subscriptions are governed by Apple's terms and the Apple Privacy Policy.

06Ratings, crash reports and App Store statistics

The App contains no analytics or crash-reporting code. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and aggregated statistics, such as the number of installs. This information doesn't identify you and is controlled entirely by you in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

From time to time, the App may ask iOS to show Apple's standard rating prompt, at most once per version. Apple decides whether to show it, and any rating or review you leave is handled by Apple.

07Advertising, tracking and third parties

The App shows no ads, contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites and doesn't access your advertising identifier. We don't sell, rent, share or trade any information with third parties, because we don't have any information about you to share.

08Children's privacy

The App is rated 4+ on the App Store and is suitable for players of all ages. We don't collect personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases are subject to the parental controls and Ask to Buy settings available on Apple devices.

09Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete your game data. Delete the App. Everything it stored on your device is removed together with it. The copy in your iCloud stays with your Apple Account, so reinstalling the App can bring your café back.
  • iCloud sync. Turn it off on a device with iCloud sync in the game's Settings, or sign out of iCloud on that device.
  • Subscriptions. Manage or cancel the Cat Club in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions, or with Manage subscription in the game's Settings.
  • Diagnostics. Choose whether to share analytics with developers in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

10Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) give you rights to access, correct, delete or move your personal data, or to object to how it's used. Since we don't collect or hold any personal data about you, we usually have nothing to access, correct or delete. You're still welcome to contact us with any privacy question or request, and we'll respond as soon as we can.

11Security

Game data is protected by the security of iOS and iPadOS, including app sandboxing and device encryption, and its copy by the security of Apple's iCloud. Because we never receive personal data, none of it can leak from our side.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App changes or when the law requires it. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change affects how your data is handled, we'll describe it in the App Store release notes too.
