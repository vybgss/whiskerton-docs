Playing

How do I get around the town?

The Café, the Bakery, the Beach and the Mountain Hotel stand side by side on one street. Swipe left or right to walk along the street, swipe up or down to move between floors, and pinch to see a whole building at once.

Every room has its own upgrade button: tap it for one level, or hold it to keep buying. New floors, renovations and the next building start right where they stand. The paw button opens everything else: your cats, the town, daily tasks, the shop and Settings.

How do I move a cat to another room?

Hold a cat until it lifts. The camera shows the whole building, and every room tells you what this cat would earn there. Then let go:

  • On a room: the cat starts working there.
  • On another cat: the two swap places.
  • In the attic: the cat takes a nap.
  • In the bowl: the cat is fed fish and goes up a level.

Carry a cat to the edge of the screen to take it to the next building. The stray cat by the door works the same way: drop it in a room to adopt it. To see a cat's card, hold the cat and let go without moving it.

How do I record or capture my café?

Open the game's Settings and tap Photo mode. The buttons hide so you can record the screen or take a screenshot. Tap the little eye to bring them back.

Why did my cats stop earning while I was away?

Your cats keep working while the game is closed, but offline income stops after a while. The Nap Room decides how long: 2 hours at first, and up to 6 hours once it's fully upgraded. Upgrade it with fish to let your cats work longer.

Cat Club members get offline income for up to 12 hours. Cats with the Dreamy personality add a little extra time on top.

What happens when I move to a bigger city?

Moving to a bigger city starts the café over in a new town and gives you permanent stars. Every star adds 2% to your coin income for good.

  • Starts fresh: coins, fish, station levels and opened locations.
  • You keep: your cats and their levels, renovations, gems, stars, achievements and everything you've bought.
How do stations and renovations work?

There are 20 stations across four locations: the Café, the Bakery, the Beach and the Mountain Hotel. Upgrading a station makes it earn more, until it reaches its level cap.

A renovation raises a station's level cap. Renovations take real time, and the builders work on one renovation at a time.

How do I hire and train cats?

There are 19 breeds. The 8 common breeds are hired for coins, and each new cat costs a bit more. The 6 rare breeds cost gems: each one is a known cat for a fixed price, with no surprises. Train your cats with fish to raise their level.

The 5 legendary cats are the strongest in town, with a crown and a magic glow. Each one is a known character, sold once in the App Store and yours for good. They stay as silhouettes until your café reaches their step, so new ones appear as your town grows.

Every cat has a skill (cooking, mixing, serving, gardening, fishing, tidying or guiding) and works best at a station that needs it. Its personality adds a bonus:

  • Cheerful cats raise the income of their whole location.
  • Diligent cats work harder themselves.
  • Charming cats make golden fish caught in their room pay double.
  • Dreamy cats add offline time.
  • Clever cats make training cost less fish.
What are daily tasks, the login streak and events?
  • Daily tasks are new every day and reward you with gems.
  • The login streak gives a reward for every day in a row you play, on a 7-day cycle. Miss a day and it starts again from day 1.
  • 40 achievements to collect. Each one you claim adds 1% to your coin income.
  • Golden fish swim by now and then. Tap one for two minutes' worth of coins, and every tenth one also gives gems.
  • The tourist bus stops by from time to time. Tap it for ×3 income for 30 seconds.
  • A themed day every day doubles the income of one kind of work, such as Baking Day for cooking.
  • The weekend festival adds ×1.5 to coin income on weekends.

Purchases and the Cat Club

What can I buy?

There are no ads, and everything for sale is optional. Each item shows exactly what it gives: there are no random rewards and no loot boxes.

  • Gem packs in several sizes.
  • Golden Bowl: ×2 income forever. A one-time purchase.
  • Starter Pack: gems, a rare cat (or extra gems if you already have that breed) and ×2 income for 4 hours. You can buy it once.
  • Legendary cats: five named cats with 500% to 7,500% base power. Each one is bought once and stays when you move to a bigger city.
  • Cat Club, a monthly or yearly subscription: 50 gems every day, ×1.5 to all income, offline income for up to 12 hours, and exclusive hats, scarves and glasses for your cats.

Prices are shown in the game in your local currency. Now and then, after a good moment in the game, a window may suggest one of these. It shows the App Store price and what the purchase would add to your café, appears at most twice a day and never uses countdowns.

How do I restore my purchases?

Make sure you're online and signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy, then tap Restore purchases at the bottom of the Shop, or in the game's Settings under Purchases.

The Golden Bowl, your legendary cats and an active Cat Club subscription come back. Gems and the contents of the Starter Pack are added to your saved game once, and that save is kept in your iCloud, so they come back with your café when iCloud sync is on.

How do I manage or cancel the Cat Club?

Apple handles subscriptions. To change your plan or cancel, do one of these:

  • Open the iOS Settings app, tap Apple Account (your name at the top), then Subscriptions, and choose Whiskerton.
  • In the game, open Settings and tap Manage subscription. It's shown while you're a member.

The Cat Club renews automatically until you cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to stop the next payment. You keep the Club benefits until the period you've paid for ends.

I paid but didn't get what I bought.

Make sure you're online, close the game and open it again, or tap Restore purchases. If it still hasn't arrived, email us with the date of the purchase and the order ID from Apple's receipt email, and we'll help.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds, so we can't issue refunds ourselves. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Sound and language

How do I turn off the sound or music?

Open the game's Settings. It has separate switches for Sound effects, Music and Haptics.

Silent mode on your iPhone (the Ring/Silent switch or the Action button) mutes the game too.

How do I change the language?

The game speaks English, German, French, Ukrainian, Spanish, Japanese and Portuguese, and follows your device's language. To pick another one, tap Language in the game's Settings, or open the iOS Settings app, choose Whiskerton and change Language there. Both places stay in sync.

Progress and devices

Where is my progress saved?

The game saves automatically on your device and keeps a copy in your own iCloud, so your café follows you to every iPhone and iPad signed in to the same Apple Account. There's no separate account to create, and the copy is stored by Apple: we can't see it.

How does iCloud sync work?

Play on one device, then open the game on another: your café is there, right where you left off. If you played on two devices without a connection, the café you played longer is kept, and gems or the Starter Pack you bought on the other device are delivered to it, so purchases are never lost.

Syncing needs you to be signed in to iCloud on both devices. To keep a separate café on one device, for example when family members share an Apple Account, turn off iCloud sync in the game's Settings on that device.

I deleted the game or got a new device. Where's my café?

Install the game, sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account and open it: your café comes back from iCloud with your cats, gems and purchases. Tap Restore purchases if the Golden Bowl, a legendary cat or the Cat Club hasn't shown up yet.

If iCloud sync was off, or the old device wasn't signed in to iCloud, the café stayed only on that device. Restore purchases still brings back the Golden Bowl, your legendary cats and an active Cat Club subscription.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The game plays fully offline. A connection is needed to buy something or restore purchases through the App Store, and for iCloud to carry your café to your other devices; changes made offline sync once you're back online.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 18 or later.
