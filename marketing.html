WhiskertonIdle Cat Cafe

Run a little cat café in a cozy small town, and let the cats do the work. They cook, brew coffee, serve guests, garden, fish, tidy up and guide tourists all on their own, earning coins and fish while you carry them to the rooms they love, collect 19 breeds and grow the café into a whole street with a bakery, a beach and a mountain hotel.

What's inside

A café full ofhard-working cats

A café that runs itself

  • Your cats cook, brew coffee, serve guests, garden, fish, tidy up and guide tourists on their own
  • Their work earns the coins and fish that grow your café
  • Between jobs they wander the floors, climb the cat tower, nap in the attic and stroll down the street, while day turns to night with your clock
  • Every cat is animated and drawn entirely in code

A living street

  • The Café, Bakery, Beach and Mountain Hotel stand side by side on one street
  • Every building is a dollhouse that grows a new floor with each room you open
  • Swipe along the street, scroll up the floors and pinch to see a whole building
  • Every room has its own upgrade button: tap for one level, hold to keep buying

Carry cats by hand

  • Hold a cat to pick it up, and every room shows what it would earn there
  • Drop it on a room to put it to work, on another cat to swap them, in the attic for a nap or in the bowl to feed it
  • Take it to the edge of the screen to carry it to the next building
  • Drop the stray by the door into a room to adopt it

Hire and train your cats

  • 19 breeds: 8 common breeds you hire for coins, 6 rare breeds for gems and 5 legendary cats with a crown and a magic glow
  • Every cat has a skill and a personality, and works best at a station that matches its skill
  • Train your cats with fish to raise their level
  • Each legendary cat is a known character, bought once in the App Store and yours for good
Cooking Mixing Serving Gardening Fishing Tidying Guiding

From café to mountain hotel

  • Upgrade and renovate 20 stations across four locations
  • Renovations take real time and raise a station's level cap
  • Open a bakery, a beach and a mountain hotel as your café grows
Café Bakery Beach Mountain Hotel

Earn while you're away

  • Your cats keep working while the game is closed
  • The Nap Room decides for how long: 2 hours at first, up to 6 hours when it's fully upgraded
  • Cat Club members get 12 hours, and dreamy cats add a little extra on top

Move to a bigger city

  • When you're ready, start over in a bigger city and earn permanent stars
  • Every star adds 2% to your coin income for good
  • Coins, fish, station levels and locations start fresh
  • Your cats and their levels, renovations, gems, achievements and purchases come with you

Something new every day

  • Daily tasks and a 7-day login streak
  • 40 achievements to collect
  • Catch golden fish, wave in the tourist bus, enjoy a themed day every day and a festival every weekend
  • Photo mode hides the buttons so you can film and share your café
Baking Day Tea Party Market Day Garden Day Rainy Day Spring Cleaning Tour Day

Speaks your language

  • The game follows your device's language, or pick one in Settings

Fair and friendly

  • No ads
  • Optional in-app purchases always show exactly what they give: no random rewards, no loot boxes
  • A special offer shows up at most twice a day, with no countdowns, and tells you what it would add to your café
  • No account and no tracking. Your café is saved on your device
